Samples

Drop a WAV, AIFF or MP3 anywhere on the page. On a drum track it replaces the last played slot; on a synth track it becomes that track's sampler, played chromatically from C4.

Save and load

The project autosaves in this browser. To move it elsewhere, export the JSON (samples included) and paste or load it back.

Options and MIDI

Each track sends and receives on its own channel (track 1 = channel 1). Incoming notes play the selected track; CC 1–4 turn dials A–D.

Writing a pattern

Tap a step
Adds the last played sound or note; tap again to clear.
Hold step + key
Adds or removes that note on the step.
Hold step + later step
Sets the note length.
Hold step + dial
Parameter lock for that step only.
REC
While playing: records what you play. While stopped: step record, one step per key.
TRACK + step
Selects one of the 16 tracks.
TRACK + key
Loads a synth engine, drum kit or effect.
TRACK + dials
Length (1–64), speed (¼–4×), swing, note length.
TRACK + OCT ±
Moves between the four bars of a long track.

Performing

SHIFT (tap)
Next parameter page.
SHIFT + keys
Punch-in effects: low octave on this track, high octave on its group.
SHIFT + steps, then a white key
Step component; steps 1–10 choose its value. Tap the key again to remove.
SHIFT + STOP
Clears step components on the track.
PROJECT + step
Pattern 1–16 (queued while playing). Add SHIFT to chain.
MIX + step
Mute. MIX + dials: volume, drums, synths, fx return.
TEMPO + dials / keys
BPM, swing, metronome, record quantize; tap keys for tap tempo.
Double-tap a modifier
Latches it, so a mouse can do two-handed moves.
