Workflow

  • Pick a track, then a mode. The screen, the four encoder cards and the strip below all follow that pair.
  • Seq: tap steps, then play keys or pads to fill the selected step. Shift-click or Multi-select several steps to add step components.
  • With a step selected, Sound cards write parameter locks for that step.
  • Sample resamples the mix or a track, loads a file, or copies the tape; play it on any track or as eight drum slices.
  • Tape records four tracks in time with the sequencer and exports a .zip of WAV files.
  • Undo reverts the last edit; pressing it again redoes it.

Open-source algorithms used here

  • Mutable Instruments Plaits (Emilie Gillet, MIT): kick, tom, snare and hi-hat models; modal partial stretching; the 17 fixed chord shapes; the formant-bank structure of the Vowel engine.
  • DaisySP (Electrosmith, MIT): hi-hat oscillator ratios; the hardware port map.
  • Open303 (Robin Schmidt, MIT): Acid engine behaviour — saw/square, 24 dB filter, accent, slide.
  • Teensy Audio Library (PJRC, MIT): Karplus-Strong string and the triangular wavefolder.
  • Freeverb (Jezar, public domain): reverb impulse response.
  • Toussaint, “The Euclidean Algorithm Generates Traditional Musical Rhythms”: Euclid writer.
  • Krumhansl–Kessler key profiles, as in FastLED key detection (MIT): Brain.

Patterns, sounds and the sample (up to 12 s) are kept in this browser; tape audio is not. Use Mixer → Export project to move a session between devices.
