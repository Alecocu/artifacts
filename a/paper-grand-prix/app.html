      How to play

      1. On your turn, drag your car to where you want it to land and let go. The move is made as soon as you release.
      2. You can move in any of the 8 directions: along a grid line or corner to corner at 45°. Always one straight line, always ending on an intersection.
      3. The distance has to match your gear. Each turn you can shift down one, hold, or shift up one, between 1st and 6th. Everyone starts in 1st.
      4. Two cars can't end a move on the same point, but you can drive through one.
      5. Red and white kerbs on the corners count as track. Leave the track and you restart from the point where you went off, in 1st gear. Go off three times and you're out.
      6. With the clock on, your time only runs on your turn. If it hits zero, you're out.
      7. When the first car crosses the line after the last lap, everyone still to move in that round gets one last move. Among cars that cross in the same round, the highest gear wins. Same gear: fewest moves wins.
      8. Pinch to zoom, drag the map to look around.
